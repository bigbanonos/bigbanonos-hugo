## How the knockout round works

Six group winners advance, plus one wild card from each conference: the best record among teams that didn't win their group. Point differential breaks a lot of ties, so a blowout can matter as much as a win.

## East

**Group A: Magic 3-1, Raptors 2-1, Pistons 2-1, Nets 1-2.** Orlando is done playing and needs both Detroit and Toronto to lose. Detroit is +31 in point differential against Milwaukee, which has already been eliminated, so a big Pistons win could decide it.

**Group B: 76ers 2-1, Heat 2-1, Cavaliers 2-2, Pacers 1-2, Knicks 1-2.** All five can still get in, but Philadelphia controls it. The Sixers hold the tiebreaker over Miami and Cleveland, so a win in Indiana sends them through.

**Group C: Celtics 3-0, Hawks 2-1.** Boston has the best point differential in the Cup at +68. Beat Atlanta at home and the group is theirs. Atlanta wins it by winning in Boston.

## West

**Group A: Mavericks clinched.** Denver is the only other team alive, and it needs a big win in Utah to have a wild-card shot.

**Group B: Timberwolves 3-0, Grizzlies 2-1.** The cleanest game of the night. Whoever wins in Minneapolis wins the group. If Memphis wins, both teams are 3-1 and the Grizzlies own the head-to-head.

**Group C: Spurs, Trail Blazers, Warriors and Lakers, all 2-1.** Four teams, two games, one group. The Spurs host the Lakers and the Blazers host the Warriors. Each team wins the group with a win plus a loss by the right team, and the other 3-1 team sweats out the wild card. Wembanyama vs. Dončić is the last game of the night.

## Why it matters

The knockout round starts in December, and the Cup ends Dec. 11. For Friday, this is the rare regular-season night where every possession in five different games actually counts. Watch the free throws in the fourth quarter.
