The No. 1 prospect in the 2027 NBA Draft plays the day after Thanksgiving. The No. 4 plays on it.

You do not need to follow college basketball for this. Feast Week puts the best teams in the country on neutral courts in Maui, the Bahamas, Fort Myers and Las Vegas, against each other, on national TV, almost every night. Forty-five games this week include at least one player on [Tankathon's big board](https://www.tankathon.com/big_board).

Here is where they are.

## What channel is Arkansas vs. Michigan State on?

CBS, Thanksgiving Day, 4:30 p.m. ET. Free over the air.

It is the best game of the week for NBA eyes. Arkansas has No. 4 Jordan Smith Jr., a 6-foot-2 guard from Washington, D.C., and Miikka Muurinen, a 6-11 Finn who left home at 15 for a Madrid academy, then prep schools in Kansas and Arizona, then a season at Partizan Belgrade before landing in Fayetteville. Michigan State has five players on the board.

## The essentials

* **Thursday, Nov. 26, 4:30 p.m. ET:** Arkansas at Michigan State, CBS. No. 4 Jordan Smith Jr.
* **Friday, Nov. 27, 5 p.m. ET:** Villanova at Kansas, ESPN2. No. 1 Tyran Stokes vs. No. 12 Luigi Suigo.
* **Wednesday, Nov. 25, 7 p.m. ET:** UConn at Duke, Prime Video. Eight board prospects, the most of any game this week.
* **Monday, Nov. 23, 11:30 p.m. ET:** Colorado State vs. Arizona, ESPN2, Maui Invitational. No. 2 Caleb Holt.
* **Tuesday, Nov. 24, 8 p.m. ET:** Texas Tech vs. Louisville, ESPN, Players Era. No. 9 Obinna Ekezie Jr.

All times Eastern. Tournament second-round and title games depend on results, so only openers are listed. Check before tip.

## Tyran Stokes is the story

The top prospect on the board has had four high schools: Prolific Prep in Napa, Notre Dame in Sherman Oaks, and Rainier Beach in Seattle, where he enrolled last November, after starting out in his hometown of Louisville. He is a 6-7 freshman at Kansas.

On Friday he gets a 7-foot-4 problem. Luigi Suigo grew up in Tradate, Italy, came through Varese and Olimpia Milano, played last season for Mega Basket in Serbia, withdrew from the 2026 draft and enrolled at Villanova.

## Basketball without borders

Four countries play for Illinois: Montenegro's David Mirkovic, Estonia's Stefan Vaaks and Croatia's Tomislav and Zvonimir Ivisic, twins. They play Monday on BTN and Friday on FS1.

Arizona has Germany. Ivan Kharchenkov was born in Moscow, raised in Bavaria and spent eight years in FC Bayern Munich's system before Tucson. Lithuania's Motiejus Krivas, 7-2, came from Zalgiris Kaunas. They open in Maui with Caleb Holt, the No. 2 prospect, and Kharchenkov is No. 8 himself.

When the 2028 Olympics come to Los Angeles, the German, Lithuanian, Italian and Croatian rosters will draw from players in this list.

## The St. Louis kid

No. 3 on Tankathon's board: Quentin Coleman, 6-4, a freshman at Illinois, from O'Fallon, Illinois, by way of Principia School in Town and Country, Missouri. Monday, 8 p.m., BTN, against Winthrop.

## Same high school, different sides

Jordan Smith Jr. and Duke's Patrick Ngongba II both went to Paul VI Catholic in northern Virginia. They play on different days, but both play on national TV this week.
