The NBA doesn't stop for it. Most of the world does.

From Thursday through Monday, national teams play the fifth of six qualifying windows for the 2027 FIBA World Cup in Qatar. NBA players are in the middle of their season, so the rosters are built from G League players, EuroLeague players and longtime pros. The names are smaller, and the games count.

## What time is USA vs. Brazil?

**Friday, Nov. 27, at 6:10 p.m. ET,** in São Paulo.

Then **Colombia on Monday, Nov. 30, at 8:10 p.m. ET,** in Cali.

## What channel is Team USA on?

Both games stream in the U.S. on **HBO Max** and on **BasketballTV on DAZN**, FIBA's streaming service (it used to be called Courtside 1891). No cable channel.

## The essentials

* **Team USA:** 7-1, first in Group E
* **Chasing:** Dominican Republic 6-2, Brazil 6-2, Mexico 5-3, Colombia 3-5, Chile 2-6
* **Coach:** Terry Stotts
* **Roster:** Josiah Allick, Mark Armstrong, Buddy Boeheim, Pedro Bradshaw, Jalen Crutcher, Hunter Dickinson, Tim Frazier, Haywood Highsmith, Dillon Jones, Kylor Kelley, Eli Pemberton, Cole Swider
* **At stake:** The top three in each group go to Qatar, plus the best fourth-place team. Win this week and the U.S. is all but in. If not, it gets another shot in February against Chile and Brazil.

## Who are these guys?

Nine of the 12 have played in the NBA. Haywood Highsmith played in the 2023 Finals with Miami. Tim Frazier has spent a decade in the league. Hunter Dickinson was a Michigan and Kansas star. Buddy Boeheim is Jim Boeheim's son.

Pedro Bradshaw is the constant. He has played in three of the four windows, and the U.S. went 6-0 in them.

## Thursday's results

Thanksgiving delivered.

* **Lithuania 97, Serbia 78.** Lithuania was 4-4 and in danger of missing its first World Cup since 2002. Dainius Adomaitis came back as coach and his team ran Serbia out of Klaipėda. Margiris Normantas scored 24 on 7-of-9 shooting and went 6 for 6 at the line. Gytis Radzevičius added 19 on 7 of 8. Alen Smailagić had 24 for Serbia, 10 of them from the line.
* **Italy 86, Türkiye 72.** Türkiye's unbeaten run ends at 8-0. Nico Mannion had 17 on 7 of 11, and Momo Diouf had 17 and 9 rebounds. Türkiye made 5 of 22 threes.
* **Georgia 92, Greece 88 (OT).** Georgia came back to win in Greece. Marcquise Reed scored 30, and Tornike Shengelia played almost 44 minutes for 25 points and 11 rebounds, 11 of 13 at the line. Greece shot 35 free throws and still lost. Kostas Antetokounmpo had 4 points in 21 minutes; Thanasis played 5 minutes.
* **Iceland 113, Bosnia and Herzegovina 101.** Elvar Friðriksson had 30 points and 10 assists, with 6 of 7 from three. Iceland made 19 threes.
* **Spain 93, Montenegro 62.** Spain's three-game skid is over. Pierre Oriola had 16. Montenegro made 3 of 21 threes.
* **Portugal 95, Ukraine 90.** Rafael Lisboa scored 26, and Diogo Brito added 20 on 7 of 8.

**Asia:** Saudi Arabia beat China 82-80, the Saudis' first win over China in more than 30 years. Japan won at Lebanon 86-82, and Korea beat Qatar 74-52.

**Americas, Thursday night:** Uruguay vs. Canada (5:10 p.m.), Argentina vs. Bahamas (7:40) and Panama vs. Puerto Rico (8:40).

## The weekend's must-watch games

* **Sunday, Nov. 29, 12 p.m. ET · Spain vs. Greece.** A rematch of Greece's 108-106 overtime win in August. Both are coming off Thursday swings in opposite directions.
* **Sunday, 1 p.m. ET · Serbia vs. Italy.** After Thursday, both are 6-3 behind Türkiye. Serbia won the first meeting 76-64.
* **Sunday, 4:10 p.m. ET · Argentina vs. Canada.** Canada came into the window 8-0.
* **Monday, Nov. 30, 1:30 p.m. ET · Germany vs. Poland.** The reigning world champions beat Poland 96-94 in August.

## Where to watch everything else

Every qualifier streams on **BasketballTV on DAZN.** Some games also air on local partners; check the listing on [FIBA.basketball](https://www.fiba.basketball) before tip.

## Why bother?

Because the 2027 World Cup and the 2028 Olympics will be built from these results. The guys playing this week are holding the door open for the NBA stars who show up next summer.
